Reporting a calculation issue

To help us reproduce a calculation, include the selected grade, basic salary, housing category, eligible-child count, age group, GPF rate, and salary stage.

Protect your privacy

Please do not include your name, employee ID, department, payslip, or other personal or employment-identifying information.

Official entitlements

PayScaleBD is an independent, unofficial calculator and cannot issue or change an official pay fixation. Confirm entitlement questions with your accounts office or the appropriate government authority.

An important note

A helpful estimate, not an official fixation.

PayScaleBD is an independent, unofficial calculator. It is not affiliated with, authorized by, or endorsed by the Government of Bangladesh.

Results are estimates—not an official pay fixation, payslip, or proof of entitlement. Confirm amounts and eligibility with the appropriate accounts office or government authority.
